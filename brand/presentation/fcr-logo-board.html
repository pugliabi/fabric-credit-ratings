What we heard

The brief

An independent site that grades each Microsoft Fabric item the way a credit agency grades a bond, from AAA down to C, scored by Microsoft MVPs and practitioners. The mark should feel trustworthy, precise and independent, live in the site's Fabric green, and work as a 16 px favicon.

trustworthypreciseindependentcalm

Success: reads as a rating at a glance, ties to Fabric without copying Microsoft's branding, recognisable at 16 px, works in one colour.

Final design

Threaded A

The top grade's A, with a crossbar woven over one leg and under the other: the rating and the fabric in one letter.

  • The A stands for the AAA end of the scale without spelling out a grade
  • The over-and-under crossbar is a woven thread, a quiet nod to Fabric
  • Built from straight cuts on a 256 grid, with a pixel-snapped cut for favicons
  • The wordmark's baseline sits on the crossbar's bottom edge, so symbol and name lock together
